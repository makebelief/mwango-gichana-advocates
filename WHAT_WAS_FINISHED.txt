IMPORTANT CONTACT NOTE:
A verified public firm phone / WhatsApp number was not found in the supplied materials or public research. The rail therefore routes those two actions to the Contact page rather than invent a number. When the verified number is available, set FIRM_PHONE in assets/js/site.js (digits only, e.g. 2547XXXXXXXX). The WhatsApp and tel: links will activate automatically.
